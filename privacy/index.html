privacy policy

last updated: july 15, 2026

hi, i'm jagger. i run a network of music pages and i work with independent artists to share their music. this page explains what information i collect through my forms and how i use it. i keep it simple because it is simple.

what i collect

when you fill out one of my forms (on this site or through an ad), i may collect:

  • your name or artist name
  • your email address
  • your phone number
  • a link to your song and any details you choose to share about it

how i use it

i use your information for one thing: to get in touch with you about your music and about featuring it on my pages. that's it. i may contact you by email or by phone.

what i don't do

i do not sell your information. i do not share or trade it with anyone for their own marketing. i don't use it for anything other than talking to you about your music.

how it's stored

if you submit through an ad, your information is collected through the advertising platform and passed to me. i keep your details only as long as i need them to be in touch with you, and you can ask me to delete them at any time.

your choices

you can ask me to see, update, or delete the information i have about you, or ask me to stop contacting you. just email me and i'll take care of it.

contact

questions about your information? email me at jaggerman068@gmail.com and i'll get back to you.
